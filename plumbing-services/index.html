How To Decide Which Service You Need

If water is actively pooling, a pipe has burst, or you have no hot water at all, that's an emergency call regardless of the time of day. If a drain is slow but not backed up, or you're noticing gradually declining water pressure, those are real problems worth addressing but not ones that require calling us at 2 a.m.

When you're not sure which of the services above fits your situation, the fastest path is still to call and describe what you're seeing. A five-minute conversation about symptoms usually gets you pointed at the right fix faster than trying to self-diagnose from a service list.

It's also fine to call about more than one issue at once. A lot of our Sun City calls end up covering two related problems, low water pressure plus a water heater that's due for replacement, for example, and it's usually more efficient to have both looked at during the same visit.

We'd rather spend a few extra minutes on the phone up front than send a technician out for the wrong job.
