Emergency Leak Detection in Sun City, AZ

A water bill that's climbing with no obvious cause, a warm spot on the floor, or a meter that keeps spinning with everything in the house turned off, these are the calls where electronic leak detection finds what a visual inspection can't.

Signs You Might Have A Hidden Leak

The clearest sign is a water bill that jumps without a clear reason, or a water meter that keeps moving even after every fixture in the house is shut off.

Inside the house, a warm patch on a slab floor usually points to a hot water line leaking underneath it, while a damp spot on a wall or ceiling that isn't near any visible plumbing often means a supply line leak somewhere behind it.

Outside, an unusually green or soggy patch of yard with no obvious sprinkler cause can mean the leak is in the service line between the meter and the house.

Why Leak Detection Is Common Work In Sun City

A meaningful share of Sun City's original homes still run copper or galvanized supply lines installed directly under a slab foundation in the early 1960s, and once a line that age develops a pinhole leak, it's sealed inside concrete where you can't see it.

The caliche soil under most of the community shifts unevenly with Arizona's extreme summer-to-winter temperature swings, which puts real stress on where a supply line penetrates the slab and is a common location for the kind of leak we trace here.

Because these leaks are invisible from the surface, guessing where to open the slab usually means unnecessary demolition, which is exactly what electronic leak detection is built to prevent.

A leak that goes unaddressed for months can also soften the soil beneath a slab section over time, which is one more reason an early, accurate diagnosis matters more here than in a market without caliche's specific behavior underfoot.

What Happens After We Find The Leak

Once the leak is located, we walk you through the real options, a point repair, rerouting the line, or, if this is the second or third leak on an aging system, whether whole-home repiping is the more cost-effective long-term move.

We'll never recommend opening a slab further than necessary just to be thorough. The goal of electronic detection is to make the repair as targeted and minimally invasive as the situation allows.

If the leak is under tile or another finished surface, we'll talk through what restoration work is likely needed afterward so there are no surprises once the plumbing repair itself is finished.
