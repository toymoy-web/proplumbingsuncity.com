Signs Your Home Needs Repiping

Recurring leaks in different spots, not the same joint failing twice, is the clearest sign that the whole line is corroding from the inside rather than one bad section.

Water pressure that's dropped gradually over years, rather than suddenly, usually means mineral scale and rust have been narrowing the inside diameter of the pipe for a long time.

Discolored water, especially a brownish tint right after the water's been off for a while, is a classic sign of rust flaking off the inside of an aging galvanized line.

Why Repiping Comes Up So Often In Sun City

Sun City is one of the oldest planned communities in this network's service area, built out from the early 1960s, and a real share of its original homes were plumbed in galvanized steel, a material with a realistic 50-to-70-year service life that's now fully in the rearview for the community's oldest sections.

Once galvanized pipe starts corroding from the inside, the damage isn't visible from outside the wall, which is why recurring leaks and slowly dropping pressure are usually the first real warning signs rather than a single dramatic failure.

Combined with the West Valley's hard water, which accelerates mineral buildup inside any metal pipe, a lot of Sun City's original supply lines are corroding faster than the same-age pipe would in a softer-water market.

We see this most clearly on homes where a repair has already been done once or twice on the same galvanized run, since patching one section rarely buys more than a year or two before the next nearby joint fails the same way.

What To Do Before Repiping Begins

Confirm It's Galvanized

Have us confirm the pipe material before assuming a repipe is needed. Some sections may already be copper or PEX from a prior repair.

Check For Active Water Damage

If a current leak has caused visible water damage, get that assessed separately so it's documented before repair work begins.

Ask About Code

New supply lines are installed to current Uniform Plumbing Code requirements, which matters most on a home whose original plumbing predates those standards.

Plan For Access Points

Knowing which walls or ceiling areas may need opening ahead of time helps you plan around the work.

What To Expect During The Work

Most Sun City repipes route new PEX or copper through attic space and wall chases wherever possible, which limits how much drywall needs to be opened compared to running everything through the slab.

Water is shut off only in stages as we finish sections of the home, and we do a full walkthrough with you once the pressure test passes, showing exactly where any patched drywall or texture touch-up is still needed.

For most Sun City homes we can keep at least one working bathroom online for the duration of the job, which we'll confirm during the initial assessment based on your home's specific layout.

Which Sun City Homes Come Up For Repiping

Repipe conversations are most common in the original section between Peoria Avenue and Grand Avenue, near Oakmont Recreation Center, the community's first, and Fairway Recreation Center.

Homes built in the later phases north of Grand Avenue are a different conversation, which is why the build date is the first thing we ask.

Replacing Galvanized Or CPVC Pipe Before It Fails

Many repiping calls start the same way. A galvanized line bursts, a copper line sprays from a pinhole or a homeowner learns that the CPVC in the attic is old and decides to act before it cracks. Pinhole leaks in copper are a sign of internal corrosion, and they tend to multiply.

CPVC can become brittle with age and heat, and attic temperatures in this area are severe. If yours is old, discolored or has cracked fittings, have it assessed before it fails.

Repiping Through The Attic Instead Of The Slab

In many slab homes, new supply lines can be routed through the attic and down the walls so the concrete is left alone. That avoids slab demolition and often shortens the project.

Whether it works for your home depends on attic access and the layout of the fixtures. We confirm both during the assessment and show you the route before you decide.
