Next Door To Sun City West And Sun City Grand

Surprise borders Sun City West and Sun City on its east side, and its center is roughly five miles northwest of where Sun City began in 1960. That short drive is why a Sun City crew can reach most of Surprise's eastern neighborhoods without a long trip across the Valley.

Sun City Grand, the Del Webb age-restricted community built from the late 1990s, sits inside Surprise. We cover it as part of this page rather than treating it as a separate market, since a call from Sun City Grand is the same kind of call we answer all day in Sun City and Sun City West.

Two Very Different Housing Eras

Surprise was founded in 1938 and incorporated as a city in 1960, the same year Sun City opened. Its original townsite was a small, one-square-mile parcel laid out for inexpensive farmworker housing, so the oldest homes in the city are in and around that townsite, where we expect older plumbing than anywhere else in Surprise.

Most of what surrounds the townsite was built in the growth that followed, especially from the late 1990s onward. A home that is 20 to 28 years old, as many in Sun City Grand are, has reached the age where its original water heater, fixture valves, and supply shutoffs commonly wear out. That is a very different conversation from the galvanized pipe story in original Sun City.

What We Handle Most Often In Surprise

In the newer neighborhoods, the calls we see most are water heaters near the end of their life, worn fixture shutoff valves, and leaks at supply connections, along with drain clogs and the occasional slab leak.

Hard water is the constant. Surprise is served by more than one water provider, including the City of Surprise and EPCOR in parts of the city, so the exact water varies by neighborhood, but the West Valley's mineral-heavy supply shortens the life of anything it flows through. A water test at your own tap tells us more than any neighborhood average.

In and around the original townsite, drain and sewer calls look more like the older-home work we do in Sun City, so we ask when a home was built before assuming either story.

Landmarks And Areas We Know In Surprise

We work along the Bell Road corridor on Surprise's eastern edge, near Surprise Stadium on Bullard Avenue, the spring training home of the Texas Rangers and Kansas City Royals, and around City Hall and Surprise Community Park at Civic Center Plaza.

The Hollyhock library branch marks the original townsite, while north Surprise has its own, much newer neighborhoods. Because the city is large and the housing varies so much, we ask where you are before making any assumption about your home's plumbing.

Before We Arrive: If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

Shut Off The Main Valve

Stops the flooding immediately while you wait for us.

Cut Power To Wet Areas

If water is near an outlet or appliance, shut off that breaker before doing anything else.

Clear The Immediate Area

Move furniture, electronics, and boxes away from standing water if it is safe to do so.
