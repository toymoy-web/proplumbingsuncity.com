Plumbing Help Around Viewpoint Lake

Viewpoint Lake is one of the most recognizable places in Sun City, and homes on the streets around it call for the same careful diagnosis as any other Sun City house. This page explains what is documented about the area and how we approach plumbing calls near the lake.

Our emergency line is open 24/7. If you have an active leak or a burst pipe right now, call (623) 301-9042 and tell us what is happening and which street you are on.

What We Know About Viewpoint Lake

According to a Del Webb Sun Cities Museum article, Viewpoint Lake opened in October 1968. The developer announced in 1967 that it would expand the community north of Grand Avenue into what became Sun City Phase 2, and the same article says 18 new model homes were introduced for Phase 2 at the 1968 grand opening.

The article describes a lake about 1,850 feet long and 950 feet wide with over two miles of shoreline once its lagoons are counted. It was filled from two wells over about 20 days, its original liner stayed in service for 55 years and was replaced in 2023, and the developer gave the lake to Sun City in 1977.

Where The Area Sits

No official boundary for a "Viewpoint Lake neighborhood" is published that we could find, so we describe the area by landmarks instead of drawing a line. The lake lies north of Grand Avenue. According to the museum's Lakeview Recreation Center article, that center sits on the northeast corner of Thunderbird Road and 107th Avenue, which was later renamed Del Webb Boulevard north of Grand Avenue, along about 1,500 feet of the lake shoreline.

If you are not sure whether your street counts as near the lake, it does not matter for service. We cover all of Sun City, so just give us your address when you call.

Housing Near The Lake

The development history above tells us the area was built out starting in the late 1960s. Beyond that, we do not claim a pipe material or a failure pattern for any street. One waterfront listing on Emerald Point records a 1975 build year, but a single listing is not a survey, so we look at each house before drawing conclusions.

What we do know is that homes of any age can have worn supply valves, aging water heaters or slow drains, and that the only way to know the condition of a particular home is to inspect it.

Water Quality In Sun City

EPCOR's published water quality report for the Sun City system (2025 edition) lists hardness between 7 and 11 grains per gallon and names groundwater from the West Salt River Valley sub-basin as the source. EPCOR updates these reports each year, so read the latest one and confirm on your bill which system serves your address.

Hardness shows up as white scale on faucets and shower glass and can build up inside water heaters over time. A simple tap test tells you more about your own home than a system-wide range does.
