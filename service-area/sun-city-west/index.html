The Copper-Era Story Behind Sun City West's Plumbing

Sun City West broke ground in 1978, roughly 18 years after Sun City itself, which puts it in a genuinely different plumbing era. Homes in Sun City West Unit 3, Unit 9, and Unit 10 were built with copper supply lines rather than the galvanized steel common in Sun City's original 1960s core, and later units like Unit 53 date to the mid-1990s.

That doesn't mean Sun City West is trouble-free. Copper supply lines now 30 to 48 years old are old enough to develop pinhole leaks from years of hard water exposure, which is a different failure mode than the galvanized corrosion story next door in Sun City, and it changes what we look for on a service call here.

Signs Of A Pinhole Copper Leak

A small, persistent damp spot on a wall or ceiling, especially one that doesn't grow quickly, is a classic early sign of a copper pinhole leak rather than a larger failure.

A water bill that's crept up gradually rather than jumped suddenly often points to a slow copper leak somewhere in the system, worth having traced electronically before it gets worse.

Blue-green staining around a fitting or valve is a specific, visible sign of copper corrosion at that connection point, and it's worth a look even if there's no active dripping yet.
