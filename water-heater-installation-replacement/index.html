What's Included

We install standard tank water heaters, tankless units, and, for homes with the electrical or gas capacity for it, hybrid heat pump water heaters, sized to your household's actual hot water use.

Every installation includes removing and hauling away the old unit, connecting new supply and venting to current code, and a pressure and temperature test before we leave.

If your home is on its original 1960s-era plumbing, we'll flag anything on the supply side, corroded shutoff valves or undersized lines, that should be addressed at the same time rather than after the new unit is already in.

We'll also point out if a water filtration or softening system makes sense to add alongside the new unit, since installing it at the same time is usually simpler and less costly than retrofitting one in later.

We're upfront about what we find either way, since the goal is the right unit for your home, not the biggest job we can sell you.

Choosing Between Tank And Tankless In Sun City

A tank water heater costs less upfront and is a straightforward swap in most Sun City homes, since the original plumbing was designed around one.

A tankless unit costs more to install, partly because Sun City's hard water makes a scale-filtration add-on worth strongly considering alongside it, but it takes up less space and doesn't run out of hot water, which matters in a household running multiple showers or loads of laundry back to back.

Because many original Sun City homes have smaller utility closets built for an older-style tank, we check clearance and venting requirements for a tankless conversion before quoting one, rather than assuming it will fit.

A heat pump water heater is worth a look too if your utility area has enough clearance and airflow, since it uses meaningfully less electricity than a standard electric tank over its life, though it needs more space around the unit than either a tank or tankless option.

Permits And Code In Sun City

A full water heater replacement, especially a tank-to-tankless conversion, typically requires a permit through Maricopa County, and we handle that paperwork as part of the installation rather than leaving it to you.

Work is done to current Uniform Plumbing Code requirements for venting and connections, which matters most on an older home where the original installation may predate the code changes since made.

We schedule the permit and any required inspection as part of the job timeline rather than leaving you to coordinate it separately, so a planned replacement doesn't turn into extra back-and-forth on your end.

Emergency Water Heater Replacement: When A Tank Fails

A tank that has failed from corrosion cannot be repaired, and a water heater that floods a garage or closet is an emergency. We dispatch 24/7, stop the water, remove the failed unit and install a replacement, usually on the same visit when a standard unit is available.

Larger changes, such as moving from tank to tankless or to a heat pump model, may need a second visit for electrical, gas or venting work. We say so at the start, so there are no surprises on the day.

Swapping Gas For Heat Pump Or Replacing Like For Like

A like-for-like replacement is the fastest path, and it keeps the existing gas, electric and venting connections. A switch from a gas tank to a heat pump unit changes the requirements, since a heat pump model needs a suitable electrical circuit, enough air around it and a place for condensate to drain.

Tell us what you have and what you are considering. We check space, circuits and venting during the estimate and give you the numbers for each option before you decide.
