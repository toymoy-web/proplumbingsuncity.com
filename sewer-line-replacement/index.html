Full Lateral Replacement

Sewer Line Replacement in Sun City, AZ

When a sewer line is compromised along most of its length rather than at one repairable spot, replacing the full lateral is the more reliable long-term fix, and it's a common conversation on Sun City's oldest clay and cast iron lines.

☎ Call (623) 301-9042

Signs You Need Full Replacement, Not A Spot Repair

Multiple problem areas found on the same camera inspection, rather than one clear point of failure, is the clearest sign the whole line has reached the end of its useful life.

Repeated backups after a prior spot repair usually means the rest of the line is following the same failure pattern that caused the first one.

A line that's collapsed or severely bellied along a long stretch is a replacement situation regardless of how the rest of the pipe looks.

What Replacement Involves

We replace the full lateral from the house to the city main or septic connection using modern PVC pipe, either through open trenching or trenchless pipe bursting depending on what the yard and existing line allow.

Trenchless pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the path of the old one, which limits how much of the yard needs to be dug up compared to a full open trench.

Why Full Replacement Comes Up In Sun City

Sun City's original clay tile and cast iron sewer laterals are now decades past the point where a handful of spot repairs meaningfully extends the line's life, and once a camera inspection shows failure at multiple points, patching each one individually usually costs more in the long run than one full replacement.

The caliche soil under most of the community affects the method more than the decision itself: trenchless pipe bursting is often the better fit here specifically because it avoids the extended digging time a full open trench through caliche requires.

A Note On Timing

If a camera inspection shows your line is trending toward full failure but hasn't gotten there yet, replacing it on your own schedule, rather than waiting for an emergency backup, is usually the less disruptive and less costly path.

A planned replacement also lets us schedule around your calendar and coordinate landscaping or hardscape repair afterward, neither of which is realistic once a line has already failed and sewage is backing up into the house.

We'll always give you an honest read on how much runway a given line likely has left based on what the camera shows, so you can make that call on your own timeline rather than ours.
