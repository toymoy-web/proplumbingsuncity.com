What We Clear

We clear clogged kitchen sinks, bathroom sinks, showers, and tubs, along with the main line that carries everything from your house to the street or the septic connection.

Most clogs come from grease and food buildup in the kitchen, hair and soap scum in the bathroom, and, in a slab home like most of Sun City's, root intrusion or scale buildup further down the main line.

We use a mechanical snake for a straightforward clog and step up to hydro jetting for a stubborn or recurring one, which is its own separate service if the line needs a full high-pressure clean.

Why Sun City's Drains Clog More Often

Sun City's original neighborhoods went in during the early 1960s, and a real share of those homes still carry their original clay or cast iron drain lines underneath a caliche-hardpan foundation. Clay pipe joints are a known weak point for root intrusion over 50-plus years, and caliche's mineral-cemented structure doesn't drain the way regular soil does, so a slow leak around a joint can sit and soften ground it shouldn't.

The West Valley's hard water adds a second layer: mineral scale narrows the inside diameter of a pipe over decades the same way it scales up a water heater, so a drain that used to handle a full load starts backing up on far less.

Between the age of the pipe and the water hardness, a Sun City home is more likely to need a real diagnosis, not just a store-bought clog remover, before the problem gets fixed.

Preventing Future Clogs

Running hot water through kitchen drains after cooking helps keep grease from setting up along the pipe wall, and a simple drain screen in the shower or tub catches hair before it builds into a clog further down the line.

For a home with the original clay or cast iron main line, a yearly camera check is worth considering even without an active clog, since it catches root intrusion or a developing offset while it's still a small, affordable fix rather than a full backup.

Chemical drain cleaners are worth avoiding on an older cast iron or galvanized system, since the harsh chemicals can accelerate corrosion on pipe that's already decades old, often trading a short-term fix for a bigger repair later.

Emergency Drain Backup: When More Than One Fixture Is Involved

A single slow sink is a nuisance. When a washing machine backs up into a tub, a toilet gurgles while another drain runs, or water rises in a shower when you flush, the blockage is usually in the main line. That is an emergency drain cleaning call, because the water has nowhere to go except the lowest fixture in the house.

Stop running water, keep people and pets away from any overflow and call us. We dispatch 24/7, clear the line first and then show you on camera what caused the backup so you know whether it will come back.

Stop Using Water

Pause the washing machine, dishwasher and showers so the line does not fill further.

Do Not Keep Plunging

If several fixtures are affected, a plunger will not reach the real blockage.

Keep Chemicals Out

Drain cleaners can sit in standing water and are unsafe for whoever clears it next.

Note What Is Backing Up

Which fixtures overflow, and in what order, helps us locate the blockage faster.

If You Have Already Tried To Clear It

Many homeowners have already tried a plunger, a store-bought snake or a chemical cleaner by the time they call. That is fine, and it helps us to know. Tell us what you used, because chemicals can still be in standing water and a snake that broke off in the line has to be located before anything else.

If a snake stopped at a certain distance, that distance is a clue. We use it, along with a camera, to find the bend, the root mass or the damaged section that stopped it. A line that keeps clogging after it has been cleared may be cracked, sagging or invaded by roots, which is a repair question rather than a cleaning one. Our sewer line repair page covers that next step.

Kitchen Sinks, Showers And Tubs Versus The Main Line

Kitchen sinks clog with grease and food residue, and slow kitchen drains often return within weeks if only the first few feet of pipe were cleared. Showers and tubs clog with hair, soap scum and mineral buildup, which is heavier in this area's hard water.

The test is simple. If only one fixture is slow, the clog is local. If two or more fixtures are slow or gurgling together, the problem sits further down the line. We start local and move down the line only as far as the problem requires.
